Tuan-Anh Le

Tuan-Anh Le, Ph.D.

Research Fellow at Mayo Clinic developing intelligent magnetic and soft robotic systems for image-guided, minimally invasive biomedical intervention.

Email: le.tuananh@mayo.edu

My research integrates magnetic actuation, dynamics and feedback control, image-based sensing, magnetic particle imaging (MPI), X-ray fluoroscopy, patient-specific digital-physical twins, and multiphysics modeling to enable precise navigation and therapy in complex physiological environments.

Key Interests

  • Magnetic and soft robotics
  • Medical microrobotics and minimally invasive vascular therapy
  • Magnetic Particle Imaging and magnetic hyperthermia
  • Image-guided robotic intervention using MPI and X-ray fluoroscopy
  • Dynamics, feedback control, computer vision, and state estimation
  • Patient-specific digital twins and multiphysics modeling
