About

Gia Minh (Kevin) Hoang

Research Fellow at Mayo Clinic — Scottsdale Campus

AI for medical imaging · Digital Twin for Organ Perfusion · Computer-aided diagnosis

Welcome to my homepage! I work at the intersection of AI and biomedical engineering. I am interested in using artificial intelligence and computational methods to address critical challenges in medicine, especially early disease classification and domain adaptation across diverse patient cohorts.

Key Interests

  • Foundation models for medical image classification.
  • Vision-language models for medical report retrieval.
  • Domain adaptation across diverse medical cohorts.
  • Medical system design for real-time data acquisition and control.
  • Digital Twin for Organ Transplantation.
